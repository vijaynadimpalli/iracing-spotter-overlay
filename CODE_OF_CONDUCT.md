# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a friendly, harassment-free space for everyone who uses or contributes to it, whatever their experience level, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to programming.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before assuming otherwise.
- Keep discussion focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, deliberately inflammatory comments or spam.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban people who behave in ways that harm the community.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/vijaynadimpalli) or open a private security/conduct report if available on the repository. Reports will be handled privately.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone is representing the project in public.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
